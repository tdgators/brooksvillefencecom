Vinyl Fence

Clean, low-maintenance privacy that shrugs off Florida weather.

Get a Free Estimate Call 813-669-4555

Vinyl is the pick for homeowners who want a finished look and don't want to think about the fence again. It won't rot, it won't rust, and it never needs paint or stain — a big deal in Hernando County's heat and humidity.

Where vinyl works best

Around the house, the pool, and the backyard, where you want privacy and a clean line. On larger properties, many owners use vinyl near the home and switch to split rail or chain link for the long back runs.

Styles and colors

Solid privacy panels, semi-privacy with small gaps, picket, and lattice-top designs, typically 4 to 6 feet tall. White, tan, and clay are the most common colors, and the color runs through the material rather than being painted on.

On sloped ground

Vinyl panels are usually stepped down a slope rather than angled. We plan the steps so the gaps under the fence stay small and the top line looks deliberate.

Quick Facts

  • No painting or staining
  • Won't rot, splinter, or rust
  • Privacy, semi-privacy & picket
  • 4'–6' heights
  • Pool-code gate hardware available
